## ***ECommerce-Analytics-Hub-360***

About This Project
This is an end-to-end SQL-based Exploratory Data Analysis (EDA) for an e-commerce retail database. It is designed to transform raw transactional data (fact_sales) and dimensional data (dim_customers, dim_products) into actionable business intelligence. By executing complex SQL queries—including window functions, common table expressions (CTEs), and advanced aggregations , this project serves as a comprehensive toolkit for understanding retail performance across all major business vectors.

Business Requirements
The core objective of this analysis was to answer critical business questions and fulfill the following requirements:

Data Integrity: Identify and clean missing or anomalous data within product categories and cost structures to ensure accurate reporting.

Executive KPI Tracking: Generate a unified, high-level summary of core metrics (Total Revenue, Volume, Active Customers, Average Order Value).

Demographic Profiling: Determine who the most profitable customers are based on age, gender, marital status, and geography.

Product Performance: Rank products and categories by sales volume and revenue contribution to identify top sellers and dead stock.

Trend & Time-Series Analysis: Track Year-over-Year (YoY) performance, seasonality, and moving averages to understand sales momentum.

Behavioral Segmentation: Classify products by pricing tiers and segment customers into distinct value groups (e.g., VIP, Regular, New) based on their lifetime spending and account longevity.

How This Analysis Drives Business Value
This SQL report moves beyond basic data extraction to provide direct strategic value to various business units:

Marketing & CRM: By segmenting customers into 'VIP', 'Regular', and 'New' tiers, marketing teams can deploy targeted retention campaigns, loyalty programs, and personalized outreach based on marital status and geographic location.

Inventory & Supply Chain: Identifying the top 5 products across multiple countries allows supply chain managers to optimize stock levels for high-velocity items (like 'Patch Kit/8 Patches' and 'Water Bottles'), preventing stockouts.

Pricing Strategy: The analysis of product cost tiers and average selling prices provides the merchandising team with the data needed to adjust pricing strategies or introduce new products in the most profitable price brackets.

Executive Strategy: The tracking of YoY growth and moving averages provides leadership with a clear view of business trajectory, removing the noise of daily fluctuations to reveal true seasonal trends.
